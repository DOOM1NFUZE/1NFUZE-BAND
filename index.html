About 1NFUZE

1NFUZE is a virtual K-pop group built around bold visuals, polished performance, and a futuristic identity. Blending striking concept design with modern pop energy, the group presents a world where music, style, and storytelling move together as one.

Sound & Identity

Defined by a sleek, high-impact sound and a visually dramatic aesthetic, 1NFUZE combines strong vocals, dynamic performance, and distinctive member presence. The group’s identity is rooted in confidence, intensity, and a digital-era edge designed to leave a lasting impression.

Current Era: CONTROL

CONTROL marks the beginning of 1NFUZE’s first era, introducing the group through a concept centred on power, presence, and precision. With a sharp visual style and a charged atmosphere, the debut era establishes the tone for the world of 1NFUZE and what is still to come.
